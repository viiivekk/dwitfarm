What began as one family's poultry shed has grown into a small, trusted farm that supplies fresh chicken, eggs and healthy parent stock across the Palghar region.

Our Philosophy

We Are Naturals — in three commitments.

  • Naturally Grown

    Our birds live free-range and stress-free, growing at their own pace for healthier meat and eggs.

  • Natural Diet

    A feed mix of jowar, maize and rice, topped up with daily foraging across open pasture.

  • Natural Ethics

    Sustainable, clean farming that respects the land as much as the livestock living on it.

Good to Know

Why Desi eggs earn a place at the table.

Eggs are one of nature's most complete foods, and our naturally raised Desi eggs are packed with nutrients that support everyday health:

  • Protein for muscle growth and repair

  • Omega-3 fatty acids for heart and brain health

  • Vitamin B12 & D for energy and strong bones

  • Zinc, iron & selenium for everyday immunity
